Renga – Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I write and save a daily haiku?

Open the app and you’ll see today’s date and three lines ready for writing.

Enter your haiku and tap Save Haiku.
Once saved, today’s haiku is locked.

You can still edit today’s haiku by returning to the main screen.
Past haikus cannot be edited.

2. Can I edit haikus from the Year screen?

Past haikus are read-only.

However, you can tap today’s haiku on the Year screen to open it and edit it.

Only today’s haiku can be edited. Once a day has passed, that poem becomes read-only and cannot be changed.

This keeps each day as a small time capsule.

3. Can I export my haikus?

Yes.

You can export your full year of haikus as a file.

At the moment, individual days or single months cannot be exported separately.

The exported file can be saved, shared, or archived.

4. How do I create a renga and invite friends?

Go to the Renga section.

Tap New Renga and give it a title.

Write the first verse.

Tap Invite.

This generates a file that you can send to a friend via Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or any sharing method.

Your friend can open the file to continue the poem.

5. How do I participate in a renga chain?

When you receive a renga file:

Save the file to your device.

Open Renga.

Go to the Renga screen.

Tap Import.

Select the file to add it to your list.

The poem will then appear in the Renga screen.

After importing:

Open the renga.

Add your verse.

Tap Invite to send the updated poem back or forward to someone else.

The renga alternates between:

3-line verses

2-line verses

Each contributor adds one verse at a time.

6. Is my data private?

Yes.

All haikus and rengas are stored locally on your device.

Renga does not require an account and does not collect personal data.

7. Why does the app send a daily reminder?

Renga may send a gentle reminder at 8pm to help you build a daily writing habit.

Notifications are optional and generated locally on your device.
